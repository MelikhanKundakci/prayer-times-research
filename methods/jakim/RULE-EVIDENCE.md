The 27 September review therefore strengthens the evidence for the **64-second Dhuhr convention**. The earlier audit had not located its explicit institutional formulation. The current [single-point model](implementation/single-point/model.mjs) uses that convention alongside 18° Fajr and the documented rounding directions. Its **floor-to-seconds stage, fixed solar-coordinate sampling times, and final zone point sets** remain reconstruction choices. None of the sources certifies this entire combination as present production behavior.

## A separately declared local point composition

The [Sunni local catalogue](../../core/local/SUNNI.md) can combine modern 18° Fajr and Isha, the factor-one noon-shadow rule, and the flat −50′ horizon with a continuously evaluated SPA point calculation. Dhuhr adds 64 elapsed seconds to transit before upward minute rounding; other prayer starts round upward and sunrise rounds downward. There is no additional uniform two-minute margin or floor-to-seconds intermediate stage.

This ordering combines the published Dhuhr formula with the separately published rounding directions. It is an explicit project composition, not evidence of the hidden service's floating-point or rounding order. The −50′ threshold already includes the conventional 34′ refraction term: applying another refraction correction would change the recipe. The point engine's sea-level and solar-provider conventions remain explicit project choices. Numerical date ranges and a Malaysia coordinate/time-zone support box are software restrictions, not official national boundaries or proof of historical applicability.

A precise GPS point and a zonal timetable are distinct outputs. Selecting a local point cannot replace extrema across a declared zone while preserving the same timetable semantics. Point accuracy and zonal safety policy need separate evidence; a universal added eight minutes is also not a substitute for recalculating the documented angle.

Seconds should describe the underlying calculation only. They must not bypass the explicit publication rounding or imply that the site's illustrative margin is a measured second-level correction. The unresolved 2027 Selangor boundary transition and unconfirmed SGR01/KDH03 point hypotheses remain documented in the [method README](README.md).
