# Why the default matches 0 of 2,010 new-season clock values

The default's **0/2,010 exact matches is real for the declared comparison**, but it does not mean its clocks are hours or days wrong. On Istanbul's 335 not-previously-scored dates in the 2026 annual calendar, every one of the six displayed events differs by one to four minutes; 935/2,010 are within one minute. The residuals have consistent event-specific directions: model Fajr is 1–2 minutes later, sunrise 2–3 minutes later, Dhuhr/Asr/Isha 1–2 minutes earlier, and Maghrib 2–4 minutes earlier. This pattern points to a mismatch in the shared Temkin/horizon/astronomy recipe, not a single clock-zone shift. The calendar rows were compared on their printed Istanbul civil date with `Europe/Istanbul`; the source and model both use UTC+03:00 for these 2026 dates. The primary-date comparison does not wrap clocks modulo 24 hours.

## What remains unidentified

The public source gives useful inputs—Fajr at 19°, first Isha at 17°, Asr shadow factors, a common Temkin, event-time MICA declination/equation-of-time, and a minute-rounding statement—but not enough operational detail to reproduce every output. The missing details include the exact current calculation point and elevation, MICA version/settings and time/coordinate frame, how the table and caution are combined in the current program, the exact horizon implementation, and the rounding operator/tie handling. The same observed minute can be produced by different combinations of those inputs; the fitted table-plus-caution result cannot identify which combination the publisher uses.

The annual comparison has no unresolved midnight Isha values or date-scale outliers. Thus source-row event-date ambiguity is not the explanation for this particular Istanbul 2026 zero-exact result. It remains relevant to other locations and calendars.

## Holdout status

The 335-date 2026 seasonal check was held out from selection of the original 12 variants. It is already exposed, including to later source-driven counterfactuals, so it cannot serve as a blind test of those later readings. For the conditional table-plus-caution hypothesis, a complete 2027 forecast was frozen before one bounded search for an ordinarily advertised Istanbul 2027 annual calendar. The search found no qualifying source; no 2027 calendar values were requested or observed. This is only a bounded availability result, not proof that no such calendar exists elsewhere. No unseen source holdout currently supports promoting any candidate or changing the public default.

The most useful evidence would be a reproducible current MICA calculation with intermediate values, the operational height/location and explicit table/caution/horizon/rounding instructions, followed by an independently available annual calendar that has not yet been viewed. Until then this method remains a negative research reconstruction, not a supported prayer-time source.
