# Diyanet rules in the local point profile

Reviewed 26 September 2026. This audit separates primary-published criteria from the point model's conventions and unresolved production details. It adopts **no new formula, offset, or rounding rule**. It reports no new calendar comparison or source-clock data. A matching criterion does not prove religious onset, institutional equivalence, or observed accuracy.

The local profile computes solar events at supplied coordinates and then applies the listed margins. The historical calendar reconstruction is a separate method with different sampling and seasonal hypotheses; neither is treated here as a verified Diyanet production algorithm.

The northern ordinary-only guard may select a real −16° crossing when its strict annual and daily checks pass. | This bounded guard does not recover the complete northern seasonal method. Summer absence and unresolved transition cases remain blocked. The archived detail and newer activity-report summary have different dates/scopes; neither supports a southern mirror. |

The Diyanet Board's [calendar-work explanation](https://fetva.diyanet.gov.tr/tr/faaliyetler/2020-2025/ibadet-vakitleri-dini-gun-ve-gecelerin-tespiti/namaz-vakitleri-takvim-calismalari) says coordinates are used for a settlement's daily Sun positions, and that missing city coordinates are added to its database. Diyanet's [app description](https://mobil.diyanet.gov.tr/mobile/web/uygulamalar/namazvakti.html) also advertises times for the user's current location. These statements support both settlement-calendar and location-based services; they do **not** reveal the exact coordinate, elevation, horizon treatment, or whether a particular app result directly evaluates raw GPS coordinates. The Temkin explanation says a single locality time must accommodate east-west and low-high differences. Therefore a precise user location alone is not evidence that Diyanet's published margins should be removed.

## Model and improvement decision

The −50′ horizon, continuous root solver, chosen SPA/USNO provider and its time-scale assumptions are software conventions, not published Diyanet rules. Diyanet's explanatory sources do not provide a complete production calculation trace or minute-rounding algorithm. Displayed minute differences cannot isolate the cause without paired source coordinates and unrounded values. Numerical seconds are model precision, not a promise of second-level observed timing.

No ordinary per-event formula or margin change is justified by the inspected evidence. The factor-one Asr criterion and +4 margin are already present; factor two would contradict the stated Diyanet calendar choice. Prior diagnostic evidence is negative, not a fresh holdout: [GLOBAL-ASR-GEOMETRY](GLOBAL-ASR-GEOMETRY.md) tested a source-motivated continuous Asr alternative on 47 already-known annual calendars and rejected it after exact Asr matches fell from 15,553 to 6,447 of 17,155. [GLOBAL-NUMERICS](GLOBAL-NUMERICS.md) found no corpus-wide win from floor/ceiling rounding, NOAA UTC00, or local-mean-midnight sampling on 50 already-exposed annual calendars. [INTERMEDIATE-VALUES](INTERMEDIATE-VALUES.md) records published examples that do not identify a rounding threshold or complete ephemeris. None is a prospective holdout or evidence for a new runtime rule.

Keep raw geometry separate from selected margin-adjusted events, and describe each profile by its explicit rule IDs and limits. A future change needs a newly identified source rule or independently identifying evidence; matching a few displayed minutes is insufficient.
