# Apia 2027: a frozen Pacific dateline transfer check

The unchanged, fully offline southern Diyanet reconstruction matches **1,519 of 2,190** displayed Diyanet minutes in Apia, Samoa in 2027. All **671** other values differ by exactly one minute: 296 model-earlier, 375 model-later. No source or model event is missing, no source `00:00` occurs, and no predicted local event date differs from the printed row date. The independently checked UTC comparison does not use modulo-24 clock matching.

## Predeclared point and calendar

The complete annual forecast, model hashes, city identity rule and date interpretation were frozen at **2026-09-25 15:19:31 UTC**, before finding an official Apia city ID or reading Apia times. The independent point is the [Government of Samoa Ministry of Finance headquarters in central Apia](https://www.mof.gov.ws/contact): **13.830099455°S, 171.767864803°W**, timezone `Pacific/Apia`. This is a city proxy, **not** a verified Diyanet production point or horizon. The [existing southern calculator](implementation/south/candidate.mjs) supplied all 365 × 6 event outputs, with no fitted Apia offset.

After the freeze, the [public Diyanet Samoa city list](https://namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr/assets/locations/SAMOA.json) identified `APIA`, city **16182**. The [official Apia page](https://namazvakitleri.diyanet.gov.tr/tr-TR/16182/apia-namaz-vakitleri) identified `SAMOA` and provided 365 ordered 2027 rows. The existing strict parser and an independent annual-table parser agreed on every cell. The public pinned-IANA [`compareCalendars`](../../validation/index.mjs) utility resolved each nonzero clock on the printed date, and an independent Python `zoneinfo` recount reproduced all UTC residuals. Every source clock had one legal UTC instant. The separate conditional next-day-Isha mode is identical in this case.

This passes the predeclared within-one-minute clock transfer gate across a Pacific civil-time boundary, but it is **not exact Diyanet reproduction**. Mixed residual signs reject a simple global one-minute shift. The printed minute table cannot determine Diyanet's original seconds, coordinate, astronomical kernel or rounding order. The source-date comparison has no date error in this cohort, but the page does not publish event-specific UTC instants.
