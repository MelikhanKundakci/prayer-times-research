# Worldwide Diyanet reconstruction: current evidence and release boundary

**A fully local Diyanet-inspired calculator is already executable in several latitude domains. An exact, notification-ready reproduction of Diyanet's city calendars everywhere is not established.** Matching a printed minute at an independently chosen city point does not identify the publisher's production point, date semantics or religious policy for unavailable twilight.

Offline reverse engineering continues from the existing archive without requiring new institutional responses or live APIs. The [shared noon inverse study](SHARED-NOON-INVERSE.md) fits two common annual coefficients and static city nuisance terms on 2026, then scores 2027 unchanged: 4,380/4,380 fitting noons and 4,366/4,380 temporal-test noons. This remains a retrospective, source-calibrated diagnostic; the common shape alone slightly worsens original-proxy agreement and is not a new general rule.

## What the new independent calendars add

The complete, pre-frozen [Jakarta, Cape Town and Auckland 2027 control](WORLDWIDE-TRANSFER-C-2027.md) compares **6,570** official annual cells; all are within one minute, with city exact counts of **1,660**, **2,045** and **2,072** respectively. The source-free forecast revealed that the existing civil-row candidate is identical to baseline in all three cities. This is geographic and timezone coverage for the baseline, not an additional test of its date-line correction. The [Iqaluit 2027 audit](IQALUIT-2027.md) adds a high-northern DST city: **1,839/2,190** exact, all within one minute, with no source `00:00` and no printed-date mismatch under the stated interpretation. Its original yearly workbook, forecast and scoring rules were frozen or archived separately; it does not settle after-midnight Isha semantics elsewhere. A separately frozen [Kathmandu 2027 check](KATHMANDU-2027.md) adds a UTC+05:45 city proxy: **2,017/2,190** exact and all within one minute. [La Paz and Suva 2027](LA-PAZ-SUVA-2027.md) add another **4,380** compared cells, all within one minute, with **1,936** and **1,657** exact. These test geographic and zone transfer at proxies, not production-point identity. Suva's baseline and civil-row candidate are identical because `C=D` throughout its frozen year.

Earlier independently frozen calendars include [Nairobi](NAIROBI-2027.md), [Tokyo](GLOBAL-SOURCES-2026-09-25.md), [Hobart](HOBART-2027.md), [Apia](APIA-2027.md), [Anchorage](ANCHORAGE-2027.md) and the [Nuku'alofa civil-row transfer](CIVIL-EPHEMERIS-DATE.md). Their locations, date conventions and exposure histories differ. Do not sum them into one advertised worldwide accuracy percentage. The Apia/Nuku'alofa evidence supports an **opt-in** date-line hypothesis; the new three-city control changes no clocks. Anchorage's attractive within-one-minute result depends on a **conditional next-day Isha interpretation** that the publisher has not defined.

A separate [18-point model-invariant grid](INVARIANT-GRID.md) checks **13,140 model-days** in 2027: no UTC/ISO mismatch, adjusted-noon date error, adjacent-order reversal or exception in the tested low/southern routes. It is not an official source comparison. High-southern twilight remains a different kind of gap: using a pre-existing Ushuaia city proxy, all **83 Fajr** and **75 Isha** unavailable model dates coincide with the archived Diyanet `00:00` dates. That strongly motivates withholding a midnight alarm, but `00:00` has no confirmed sentinel definition in the inspected Diyanet calendar. The model does not invent replacement prayer times.

## Why the current evidence does not identify a worldwide Diyanet algorithm

1. **A city page is not a calculation point.** The [public location-flow audit](LOCATION-SCOPE.md) shows GPS input being resolved through country/province/district to a Diyanet calendar ID. The inspected public response does not provide a complete coordinate/height catalogue, region boundaries or a GPS-to-production-point rule. Our points are documented independent proxies. Diyanet's [Temkin explanation](https://vakithesaplama.diyanet.gov.tr/temkin.php) explicitly discusses publishing one time for a settlement despite east/west and height differences.
2. **`HH:mm` does not always define an instant.** In northern sources, nonzero Isha can appear earlier than same-row Maghrib; interpreting it as next day often improves agreement, but the [event-date audit](EVENT-DATE-SEMANTICS.md) cannot prove that is the publisher's intended UTC date. Source `00:00` remains ambiguous. A clock that matches modulo a day is not safe for an alarm.
3. **The published astronomical rules are incomplete for exact reproduction.** The [intermediate-value audit](INTERMEDIATE-VALUES.md) did not identify current per-city production points, complete ephemeris inputs, northern seasonal transitions or exact rounding order. A [coordinate-feasibility check](FEASIBLE-COORDINATES.md) mathematically rejects a point-only exact repair under the retained UTC00 recipe for some cities. A separate [365-day La Paz/Suva noon check](LA-PAZ-SUVA-NOON-FEASIBILITY.md) reaches the same limited conclusion for each new southern calendar. [Later equation-of-time sampling](EOT-SAMPLING-TRANSFER.md) loses more known 2027 noon matches overall, and global rounding countertests also failed. Adjusting city offsets until a known calendar matches would conceal these missing rules.
4. **The domains are separate.** The current northern seasonal route is a reconstruction for 44.5–75°N. Low-latitude and southern routes use another explicit recipe; the southern route ends at 60°S and does not replace absent twilight. Neither mathematical input range nor several matching calendars proves every city worldwide.

## Offline app path

The device can compute at a supplied GPS point entirely offline, with a visible method/variant, IANA zone, actual event date, availability reason and source/version metadata. That result should be labeled an **experimental Diyanet-inspired calculation at the user's point**. An **official Diyanet city calendar** is a different claim: it requires a versioned offline city/area catalogue and evidence for which city calendar and operational point apply. A one-time research download does not introduce a runtime API dependency, but an unverified nearest-city rule is not equivalent to Diyanet's own selection.

Before enabling Diyanet-labeled notifications worldwide, require complete country/area assignment evidence, a confirmed event-date and `00:00` contract, verified production-point or region policy, and prospective full-year source comparisons across ordinary, high-northern, high-southern, DST and date-line places. Record every unavailable field and every regression. Until those conditions are met, keep automatic-notification eligibility false for these research variants, including when displayed-minute agreement looks strong.
