# Special rules: source audit and implementation boundary

Reviewed 27 September 2026. This audit distinguishes a published religious criterion, a timetable publisher's implementation, and a software preset. These are different kinds of evidence. A method name is not a denomination, an Asr factor does not determine a twilight policy, and a computationally valid estimate is not automatically an institution's adopted rule.

The review used public explanatory publications and retained source material. It did not contact institutions, request prayer calendars or APIs, or measure new calendar agreement. “Not established” below means not established by the inspected sources; it is not a claim that no guidance exists anywhere.

## Optional 45° reference: the implemented subset

The four IDs are `sunni-{mwl|egyptian}-shadow{1|2}-reference45-v1`. They are an explicit local composition of a dated proportional-night rule, not a new default or a reproduction of the latest institutional timetable. The [Saudi Press Agency council report](https://www.spa.gov.sa/497737?lang=ar&newsid=497737) cites the ninth-session resolution from Rajab 1406 / 1986; the [nineteenth-session announcement](https://www.spa.gov.sa/497571) identifies the 3–8 November 2007 session. [Dar al-Ifta fatwa 2259, 6 June 2012](https://www.dar-alifta.org/ar/fatwa/details/13433/حكم-الجمع-في-البلاد-التي-تنعدم-فيها-العلامات) reproduces the council's 48°–66° north/south scope and suggested 45° reference. It also discusses hardship permissions; this implementation deliberately does not turn hardship into a universal numerical cap.

The [runtime contract and formulas](REFERENCE45.md) specify the exact subset: retain real crossings, estimate only proven continuous summer absence within absolute latitude 48°–66°, and require actual local and reference horizons. The same-longitude ±45° point, shared timezone, adjacent-date pairing and solar conventions are explicit project choices. Tangencies and numerical failures do not trigger it. Estimates remain marked; the missing-only switch can produce a transition jump and is not smoothed. Polar horizons are not invented.

## A separate 2009 MWL-related policy remains research

The International Astronomical Center's [Arabic account](https://astronomycenter.net/latitude.html?l=ar) describes a committee-approved local relative method from 1 August 2009 for 48.6°–66.6°. Preserve existing signs except when the prayer's difference from the preceding or following day exceeds ten minutes. Compute the annual mean of sunset-to-Isha divided by sunset-to-sunrise, using only days with present, non-disturbed signs; use that fraction for replacement Isha and Fajr. At entry, move Isha five minutes earlier and Fajr five minutes later per day until within five minutes of the fraction candidate; use a corresponding return transition.

That is a different algorithm from a 45° reference night. It is not implemented by the new `reference45` profiles. Baseline angles, clock frame across DST, adjacent-night ownership, disturbance-window boundaries and return-transition ordering still need an explicit, independently tested contract. The account is primary evidence from a calculation participant, not proof of every present MWL service's backend. A later proposal must not be described as adopted without adoption evidence.

## Diyanet: avoid combining incompatible versions

The [10 June 2009 ruling](https://kurul.diyanet.gov.tr/tr/fetva/45-enlemin-otesinde-namaz-vakitleri/d681fdc5-924c-4f02-bb74-08dd1c135350) describes an 80-minute Isha limit, a March–September Fajr construction with an additional ten minutes, and a 62° reference beyond that latitude. It also acknowledges alternative qualified estimations. These are historical instructions, not missing pieces to insert into the later scheme.

The [2020–2025 activity report](https://kurul.diyanet.gov.tr/tr/faaliyetler/2020-2025/ibadet-vakitleri-dini-gun-ve-gecelerin-tespiti/ileri-enlemlerde-namaz-vakitleri) identifies the 26–27 September 2021 congress and implementation from 2023. It summarizes the northern scope as 45° N and above; the retained [detailed criteria](https://www.awqatsalah.com/sub/34/tespit-kriterleri) use 44.5°. Our [rule audit](../../methods/diyanet/LOCAL-RULE-AUDIT.md) preserves that distinction. A local interpolation or frozen-ratio interpretation remains a named project policy where the complete institutional equation is unpublished.

## Karachi and Hanafi guidance need an issuer

[Banuri Town fatwa 144707100380](https://www.banuri.edu.pk/readquestion/144707100380/25-12-2025) directly supports the 18°/18° angle pair. It does not establish that every application labelled “University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi” uses Banuri Town's full recipe or that every Hanafi community adopts it.

The separate [Wifaqul Ulama FAQ](https://www.wifaqululama.co.uk/salahtimes/) describes missing-18° Fajr choices: a last-real-day approach or the midpoint of actual sunset to next sunrise. Its app uses a modified three-day average for the former; the exact averaging dates and transfer conventions require further specification. Its [11 November 2018 meeting report](https://www.wifaqululama.co.uk/highlat/) discusses 15° Isha, several hardship alternatives and a distinct missing-twilight treatment. It explicitly records no agreed national hardship solution and leaves the southern hemisphere unaddressed. These sources support separately named future research, not an automatic worldwide Karachi fallback.

- [FCNA recommendation](https://fiqhcouncil.org/the-suggested-calculation-method-for-fajr-and-isha/): meeting dated 27–29 October 2017; the webpage also carries 2021 and 2026 metadata. The meeting date identifies the recommendation used here.

- [Kemenag Temanggung training report](https://temanggung.kemenag.go.id/bimbingan-masyarakat-islam/standar-baku-hisab-rukyat-dalam-pelatihan-perhitungan-jadwal-shalat/): page dated 24 October 2022, reporting training on 14 April 2016. This distinction is retained in the linked audit.
- [Pahang's *Asas Falak Dalam Ibadah*](https://mufti.pahang.gov.my/muat-turun-dokumen/nota-kursus-taklimat/3-asas-falak-dalam-ibadah/file), PDF pages 11–16, and [Selangor's 9 August 2024 noon explanation](https://www.muftiselangor.gov.my/2024/08/09/66-taudhih-al-hukmi-hukum-solat-pada-waktu-rembang/) support selected Malaysian components; [Selangor's FAQ](https://www.muftiselangor.gov.my/soalan-lazim/) supplies rounding directions. These do not certify the combined local recipe as an e-Solat backend.

Source-backed rules, project choices and unavailable events remain separate in the output. An astronomy test verifies the declared equations; it does not resolve religious disagreements, identify a publisher's hidden point, or demonstrate agreement with a new official timetable.
