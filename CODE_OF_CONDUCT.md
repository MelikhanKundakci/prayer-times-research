# Code of conduct

This project welcomes people of different backgrounds, communities, and levels of experience. Discuss calculations, sources, and interpretations with respect.

- Be specific about evidence and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Critique an implementation or claim rather than attacking a person or community.
- Respect differences in Islamic jurisprudence and institutional practice.
- Do not post harassment, personal information, credentials, or material you are not entitled to share.
- Assume questions are asked in good faith and help newcomers find the relevant documentation.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and limit participation when necessary. Raise ordinary research disagreements in an issue with concrete references. For sensitive concerns, use GitHub's reporting mechanisms rather than publishing personal details in a public issue.
