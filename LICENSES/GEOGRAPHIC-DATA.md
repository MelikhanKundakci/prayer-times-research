# Geographic data attribution

## GeoNames

Geographic coordinates identified as GeoNames-derived are from **GeoNames**, available under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Source: [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/) and its [data export documentation](https://www.geonames.org/export/).

The project selects individual city records, represents them in JSON or code, and adds IANA timezone identifiers and research metadata. These changes do not imply endorsement by GeoNames. Where no coordinate transformation was performed, the original published numbers are retained. Source identifiers and links accompany retained examples. GeoNames data are not relicensed under the project's MIT license.

## Other independently published points

Any retained JUPEM zone points, MyGeoName village proxy, UNGEGN city point or FMI geographic reference is separately identified in its method metadata. The GeoNames license does not apply to those sources. Their original publications and complete databases are not included. Limited numerical transcriptions and links preserve provenance; the project does not claim a general open-data license for those publications or that a general geographic point is a prayer calculation point.

For JAKIM experiments, the distinction between the published zone table, a map-corner hypothesis and a village proxy must remain visible. A source year or datum is not silently converted into a claim about present operational practice.
